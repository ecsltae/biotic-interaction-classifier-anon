\caption{One passage, several candidate pairs. Retrieval proposes a candidate when a passage names two organisms near an interaction word (bold). The sentence question gets one answer for the whole passage, so even a perfect answer accepts the third pair, two bacterial genera merely listed together. The third pair is a real benchmark item (\textsc{Test}299, graded negative) that the sentence-level model accepts and both conditioned models reject at their block-held-out thresholds; the first two pairs are illustrative. Bottom: model inputs.}

% Figure 3 (fig3_gain_by_taxa.pdf)
\caption{AUPRC by the number of entities the passage names (three-checkpoint ensembles). Biodiversity: distinct taxa that TaxoNERD~\citep{TAXONERD-KEY} finds in the passage; an argument ``is not a taxon'' when TaxoNERD does not recognise it there. BioRED: gold-annotated concepts in the sentence; BioRED has no relation term, so there is no triple-conditioned model. Triple-conditioned markers sit just below their row so they do not hide the pair-conditioned ones. Grey bars and the $\Delta$ column give the gain from adding the pair (pair-conditioned minus sentence-level). Exact values: Appendices~C and~D.}
